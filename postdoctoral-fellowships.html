Postdoctoral Fellowships

AQIRI offers opportunities for PhD holders to pursue research and scholarly publication in Islamic studies and related interdisciplinary fields.

Research Areas

  • Qur’an & Hadith
  • Islamic Law & Ethics
  • Theology & Philosophy
  • History & Culture
  • Arabic & Textual Studies
  • Religion & Society
  • Peace & Interfaith Studies
  • Economics & Governance

Fellowship Options

  • Research: Independent research towards scholarly publications.
  • Publishing: Manuscript development, editing, and publication projects.
  • Visiting: Short-term academic engagement with AQIRI.
  • Non-Resident: Remote participation in an agreed research project.

Key Details

Duration
3 months, 6 months, or 1 year, depending on the fellowship.
Mode
On-site, remote, or hybrid, as announced.
Funding
Non-resident fellowships are currently unpaid. Funding for other opportunities will be specified in individual announcements.
Eligibility
Completed PhD in a relevant field and a suitable research proposal.
Expected work
Agreed research outputs, progress updates, and a final report.

Fellows must follow academic integrity standards and AQIRI’s affiliation policy.

Application Requirements

  • Academic CV, including publications and contact details
  • PhD qualification details
  • Research proposal covering objectives, methodology, contribution, work plan, and selected bibliography
  • Writing sample
  • Preferred fellowship option, duration, and mode

Additional documents may be requested during review. Shortlisted applicants may be invited for an interview or proposal discussion.

How to Apply

Email your application to careers@aqiri.org.
