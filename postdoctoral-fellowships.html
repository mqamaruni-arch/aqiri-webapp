Advanced Research Opportunities at AQIRI

Postdoctoral
Fellowships

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute offers postdoctoral fellowship opportunities for scholars who have completed their PhD and wish to pursue advanced research in Islamic Studies and related interdisciplinary fields.

How to apply ↓

Fellowship Program

Advanced research with a clear scholarly purpose.

The fellowship program supports serious academic research, publication development, scholarly writing, manuscript preparation, journal articles, book projects, and collaborative research connected with AQIRI’s academic vision.

Applications are welcomed in Islamic Studies, Qur’anic Studies, Hadith and Seerah, Islamic law, Islamic thought, Muslim history, religion and society, humanities, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Fellowship Areas

Fields of inquiry

Focused areas for research, publication, training, and scholarly collaboration across the major disciplines of Islamic Studies.

01

Qur’anic Studies

Tafsir, Qur’anic sciences, and contemporary guidance

02

Hadith & Sunnah

Hadith sciences, classical literature, and Prophetic guidance

03

Seerah & Islamic Civilization

Leadership, institutions, intellectual history, and civilization

04

Fiqh & Islamic Law

Jurisprudence, legal theory, Maqasid, and contemporary questions

05

Islamic Thought, History & Culture

Kalam, philosophy, Tasawwuf, literature, and culture

06

Comparative Religion & Interfaith

Dialogue, religious harmony, peacebuilding, and ethics

07

Academic Publishing & Research

Peer review, manuscript development, and scholarly training

Fellowship Types

Choose the format that fits your research.

01

Postdoctoral Research Fellowship

Independent research leading to an article, monograph, book chapter, edited volume, or policy-oriented academic paper.

02

Postdoctoral Publishing Fellowship

Develop publishable work, edit manuscripts, prepare scholarly books, support journal research, or contribute to AQIRI publication projects.

03

Visiting Postdoctoral Fellowship

Collaborate with AQIRI for a limited period through research, lectures, workshops, publications, or academic projects.

04

Non-Resident Postdoctoral Fellowship

Work remotely while remaining academically associated with AQIRI for a defined research or publication project.

Program Details

Eligibility, duration, and expected work.

Duration3 months / 6 months / 1 year
ModeOn-site / Remote / Hybrid
StatusPaid / Unpaid / Partially supported, according to announcement

Eligibility

  • A completed PhD or equivalent doctoral qualification
  • A research background in Islamic Studies or a related field
  • A clear research proposal and ability to produce scholarly work
  • Commitment to academic ethics and research standards
  • Strong writing, documentation, and communication skills
  • Previous publications or research experience, where applicable

Final eligibility may vary according to the specific fellowship announcement.

Expected Outputs

  • Research article, book chapter, or research report
  • Monograph or edited-volume proposal
  • Translation or critical-edition work
  • Seminar, lecture, or workshop session
  • Academic blog or public scholarship piece
  • Contribution to AQIRI publication projects
  • Final fellowship report

Professional Expectations

Fellows must maintain academic integrity, research discipline, confidentiality where required, proper citation practices, respectful communication, and timely submission of assigned work. AQIRI affiliation should be acknowledged in fellowship outputs according to institutional policy.

Application Requirements

Prepare a complete application file.

Applicant Documents

  • Full name and contact details
  • Academic CV and PhD degree details
  • Institutional affiliation, if any
  • List of publications
  • Research proposal and statement of interest
  • Writing sample
  • CNIC / Passport copy and recent photograph
  • Recommendation letter, if required
  • Proposed duration and preferred mode

Research Proposal

  • Title and background of the study
  • Research problem, questions, and objectives
  • Methodology and primary sources
  • Expected contribution and output
  • Work plan
  • Selected bibliography

The proposal should be clear, focused, academically serious, and relevant to AQIRI’s research priorities.

Selection Process

Academic review in three stages.

01

Application Review

Applications are assessed by academic background, proposal, publication record, relevance, and project feasibility.

02

Shortlisting

Shortlisted applicants may be invited for an interview, proposal discussion, clarification, or further documentation.

03

Final Selection

Selected fellows receive official communication covering terms, duration, responsibilities, reporting, and expected outputs.

How to Apply

Submit your fellowship application.

Send your CV, research proposal, writing sample, publication list, and relevant academic documents by email. Incomplete applications may not be considered.

Email subject: Application for Postdoctoral Fellowship — Applicant Name
