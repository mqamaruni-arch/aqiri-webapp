Admission Review

  • The applicant selects the relevant department and course pathway.
  • The Admissions Office checks the submitted application and required details.
  • The relevant department reviews subject fit, background, and course capacity.
  • Final confirmation depends on department approval and administrative verification.

What to Prepare

  • CNIC/passport details and contact information.
  • Educational qualifications and current institution, if any.
  • Selected department and intended course, certificate, diploma, or workshop.
  • Short statement of purpose or academic interest.
  • Certificates, CV, photograph, and writing sample where required.
