Board of Governors

The Board of Governors provides senior academic guidance, institutional oversight, quality assurance, and scholarly direction for Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute. Its role is to support responsible research culture, academic standards, publication ethics, training quality, and institutional collaboration.

Prof. Dr. Qibla AyazBoard Member

Member/Judge of the Shariat Appellate Bench, Supreme Court of Pakistan, with extensive scholarly and administrative experience in Islamic studies, Islamic law, and contemporary religious thought. He has previously served as Chairman of the Council of Islamic Ideology, Pakistan, and has held senior academic leadership roles in higher education. His work reflects a strong engagement with Islamic jurisprudence, constitutional and legal questions, interfaith dialogue, and the application of Islamic thought to contemporary social and institutional issues.

Email: qibla.ayaz@aqiri.org

Prof. Dr. Mohd Roslan Bin Mohd NorBoard Member

Professor at the Department of Islamic History, Civilization and Education, Academy of Islamic Studies, Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, and currently serving as Head of the Department. His academic work focuses on Islamic history, civilization, Islamic Jerusalem studies, religion, and contemporary Muslim societies. He has also served in senior academic and research leadership roles, including as Deputy Director for Research and Development at the Academy of Islamic Studies. His scholarship reflects a broad engagement with Muslim intellectual traditions, civilizational studies, religious education, and contemporary issues affecting Muslim communities.

Email: mohd.nor@aqiri.org

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Khalid MasudBoard Member

Internationally recognized scholar of Islamic law, jurisprudence, and contemporary Muslim thought. He has served as Chairman of the Council of Islamic Ideology, Pakistan from 2004 to 2010, Director General of the Islamic Research Institute, International Islamic University Islamabad, and Ad Hoc Member/Judge of the Shariat Appellate Bench, Supreme Court of Pakistan. He has also been associated with leading academic institutions in Pakistan, Europe, and the Muslim world.

Email: muhammad.masud@aqiri.org

Prof. Dr. Abu Sufyan IslahiBoard Member

Distinguished scholar of Arabic and Islamic studies, associated with the Department of Arabic, Aligarh Muslim University, India. He has also served as Editor of the journal Tahzib-ul-Akhlaq and is recognized for his scholarly contributions as an author, researcher, and academic advisor to Islamic studies journals. Public profiles also mention his association with advisory/editorial boards and his recognition as a recipient of the Nuqoosh Award.

Email: abu.islahi@aqiri.org

Prof. Dr. Arshad Munir LaghariBoard Member

Professor of Islamic Studies at Ghazi University, Dera Ghazi Khan, where he serves as Chairperson of the Department of Islamic Studies. His academic work is linked with Qur'anic studies, Islamic history, Sufism, cultural studies, comparative studies, and contemporary issues in Muslim society. He has contributed to teaching, research, academic administration, and editorial/advisory work in the field of Islamic Studies, with a focus on connecting classical Islamic scholarship with modern academic discourse.

Email: arshad.laghari@aqiri.org

Dr. Bilal MasudBoard Member

Oxford doctoral graduate in Theoretical High Energy Physics and has served as Director of the Centre for High Energy Physics, University of the Punjab, Lahore. His academic work is rooted in advanced physics, particularly in the study of fundamental questions related to matter, energy, and the structure of the universe. Alongside his scientific research, he has also engaged with broader intellectual themes, including the relationship between Islam and science, metaphysics, and philosophical questions arising from modern scientific thought. His profile reflects an interdisciplinary interest in connecting scientific inquiry with deeper questions of knowledge, existence, and religious understanding.

Email: bilal.masud@aqiri.org

Governance Responsibilities

The Board supports academic planning, quality assurance, research ethics, journal and publication standards, institutional partnerships, scholarly training, and the long-term development of AQIRI programs.
