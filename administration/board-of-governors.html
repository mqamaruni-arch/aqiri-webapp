Board of Governors

The Board of Governors provides senior academic guidance, institutional oversight, quality assurance, and scholarly direction for Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute. Its role is to support responsible research culture, academic standards, publication ethics, training quality, and institutional collaboration.

Governance Responsibilities

The Board supports academic planning, quality assurance, research ethics, journal and publication standards, institutional partnerships, scholarly training, and the long-term development of AQIRI programs.

Academic Direction

Guidance for departments, research priorities, fellowships, seminars, publications, and scholarly programs.

Quality Assurance

Review of standards for supervision, coursework, assessment, certificates, editorial practice, and institutional records.

Institutional Development

Advice on collaborations, public scholarship, national and international partnerships, and sustainable academic growth.
