Board Member

Prof. Dr. Mohd Roslan bin Mohd Nor

University of Malaya, Malaysia

Profile

Prof. Dr. Mohd Roslan bin Mohd Nor is a senior academic in Islamic history, civilization, and Muslim societies. His scholarly work and academic leadership contribute to the study of Islamic civilization, historical development, Muslim communities, and cross-cultural intellectual exchange.

His presence on the Board of Governors strengthens AQIRI's international academic perspective and supports the institute's commitment to collaboration beyond national boundaries. His academic interests are closely aligned with research on Muslim history, social transformation, and the development of Islamic institutions across different regions.

Through his role, AQIRI benefits from a broader view of Islamic civilization and from guidance on how research programs can connect local scholarship with international academic conversations.

Contribution to AQIRI

At Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute, Prof. Dr. Mohd Roslan bin Mohd Nor provides guidance in research mentoring, academic networking, international cooperation, and the development of scholarly programs.

His advice is especially valuable for projects related to Islamic civilization, Muslim history, regional studies, and institutional collaboration across academic communities.
