Board Member

Prof. Dr. Qibla Ayaz

Supreme Court Shariat Appellate Bench

Profile

Prof. Dr. Qibla Ayaz is a senior scholar of Islamic Studies, Islamic law, and contemporary Muslim thought. He has served as Chairman of the Council of Islamic Ideology, Pakistan, and has contributed significantly to academic, religious, and public-policy discussions in the country.

His scholarly work reflects deep engagement with Islamic legal tradition, modern social questions, constitutional debates, and the role of religious scholarship in contemporary public life. His experience connects academic research with institutions, policy questions, and wider social concerns.

On the Board of Governors, Prof. Dr. Qibla Ayaz supports AQIRI's commitment to balanced Islamic scholarship, responsible intellectual engagement, and meaningful dialogue between classical Islamic learning and modern public challenges.

Contribution to AQIRI

As a member of the Board of Governors, Prof. Dr. Qibla Ayaz provides guidance in academic direction, research ethics, institutional policy, and scholarly development. His advice supports programs that require careful engagement with Islamic law, public policy, and contemporary Muslim thought.

His role helps AQIRI pursue scholarship that is academically responsible, socially aware, and attentive to the ethical responsibilities of religious research.

Fields of Guidance

  1. 01Islamic law, public policy, and constitutional questions.
  2. 02Contemporary Muslim thought and religious scholarship.
  3. 03Research ethics, institutional policy, and academic direction.
  4. 04Dialogue between classical learning and modern public challenges.
