Board Member

Prof. Dr. Arshad Munir Laghari

Ghazi University, Dera Ghazi Khan

Profile

Prof. Dr. Arshad Munir Laghari is a respected academic and scholar of Islamic Studies associated with Ghazi University, Dera Ghazi Khan. He brings valuable experience in university teaching, research supervision, institutional service, curriculum development, and contemporary religious studies.

His academic work reflects a strong commitment to higher education and to the development of Islamic Studies as a serious, research-oriented discipline. Through teaching and supervision, he has supported students and researchers in building disciplined approaches to religious scholarship and social engagement.

On the Board of Governors, his perspective helps AQIRI remain connected with university-level academic practice, responsible research culture, and the needs of emerging scholars.

Contribution to AQIRI

Prof. Dr. Arshad Munir Laghari supports AQIRI in academic planning, institutional development, research guidance, and training activities. His experience helps the institute shape seminars, workshops, publications, and research programs with clear academic purpose.

His contribution also strengthens AQIRI's relationship with higher education institutions and supports the institute's mission of promoting balanced, socially relevant, and methodologically sound Islamic scholarship.

Fields of Guidance

  2. 02Higher education, teaching, and research supervision.
  3. 03Curriculum development and academic planning.
  4. 04Institutional development and scholarly training programs.
