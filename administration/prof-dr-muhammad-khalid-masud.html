Board Member

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Khalid Masud

International Islamic University, Islamabad

Profile

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Khalid Masud is an internationally recognized scholar of Islamic law, jurisprudence, and modern Muslim thought. His academic leadership includes association with major institutions such as the Islamic Research Institute, International Islamic University Islamabad, the Council of Islamic Ideology, and important legal and intellectual forums related to Islamic jurisprudence and Muslim societies.

His scholarship is respected for its depth, clarity, and careful engagement with both the classical Islamic legal tradition and contemporary questions of law, society, and institutional life. He brings to AQIRI a senior scholarly perspective shaped by research, public service, and academic leadership.

On the Board of Governors, Prof. Dr. Muhammad Khalid Masud helps guide the institute toward serious, ethical, and research-based Islamic scholarship that remains attentive to both inherited tradition and modern realities.

Contribution to AQIRI

At Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute, he contributes senior academic insight to research planning, scholarly evaluation, publication standards, and the development of programs related to Islamic jurisprudence and Muslim intellectual history.

His guidance is especially valuable for work involving legal interpretation, the history of Islamic thought, contemporary Muslim societies, and the relationship between Islamic tradition and public institutions.

Fields of Guidance

  1. 01Islamic law, jurisprudence, and legal interpretation.
  2. 02Modern Muslim thought and Muslim intellectual history.
  3. 03Research leadership, scholarly evaluation, and publication quality.
  4. 04Islamic tradition, public institutions, and contemporary society.
