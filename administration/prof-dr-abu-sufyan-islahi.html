Board Member

Prof. Dr. Abu Sufyan Islahi

Aligarh Muslim University, India

Profile

Prof. Dr. Abu Sufyan Islahi is a distinguished scholar of Arabic and Islamic Studies associated with Aligarh Muslim University, India. His academic work spans teaching, authorship, editorial service, manuscript review, and the mentoring of scholars working in Islamic studies and Arabic literature.

His scholarship reflects close engagement with classical Islamic texts, Arabic literary traditions, academic writing, and the standards required for serious research publication. Through his work as a teacher, writer, editor, and reviewer, he has contributed to the development of disciplined scholarship in Arabic and Islamic Studies.

His presence on the Board of Governors strengthens AQIRI's commitment to cross-border academic cooperation and to research that remains connected with classical learning while meeting the expectations of contemporary academic publication.

Contribution to AQIRI

At Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute, Prof. Dr. Abu Sufyan Islahi offers guidance in Arabic studies, Islamic literature, academic editing, manuscript assessment, and publication quality. His experience supports the institute's work on research articles, edited volumes, scholarly books, and journals.

His advice is especially valuable for maintaining careful editorial standards, encouraging responsible citation practices, and helping research outputs meet the expectations of scholars, reviewers, and academic institutions.

Fields of Guidance

  1. 01Arabic Studies and Islamic literature.
  2. 02Academic publishing, editing, and manuscript review.
  3. 03Classical Islamic scholarship and research methodology.
  4. 04Scholarly cooperation between national and international institutions.
