Board Member

Dr. Bilal Masud

University of the Punjab, Lahore

Profile

Dr. Bilal Masud is an Oxford doctoral graduate in Theoretical High Energy Physics and former Director of the Centre for High Energy Physics, University of the Punjab, Lahore. His academic interests connect scientific inquiry, metaphysics, philosophy, and the Muslim intellectual tradition.

His scholarly outlook brings an important interdisciplinary dimension to the Board of Governors. It encourages careful reflection on the relationship between natural sciences, philosophical reasoning, ethical questions, and the intellectual heritage of Muslim societies.

Through this perspective, Dr. Bilal Masud helps AQIRI think beyond narrow disciplinary boundaries and supports programs that invite thoughtful engagement between science, religion, philosophy, and society.

Contribution to AQIRI

As a Board Member, Dr. Bilal Masud contributes to academic planning, research development, and intellectual dialogue. His guidance supports AQIRI's interest in programs that examine modern knowledge, metaphysical questions, and the ethical dimensions of science and society.

His role is especially important for research initiatives that require conceptual clarity, critical thinking, and dialogue between Islamic scholarship and other fields of knowledge.

Fields of Guidance

  1. 01Science, philosophy, and metaphysical inquiry.
  2. 02Interdisciplinary research and intellectual dialogue.
  3. 03Critical thinking in the study of religion and society.
  4. 04Engagement between modern scientific knowledge and Muslim thought.
