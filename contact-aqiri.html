Contact AQIRI

Use the official AQIRI contact channels below for academic, institutional, admissions, fellowship, publication, technical, legal, copyright, accessibility, or general queries.

General Contact Channels

  • info@aqiri.org for general information
  • programs@aqiri.org for programs
  • admissions@aqiri.org for admissions
  • careers@aqiri.org for careers
  • hr@aqiri.org for human resource matters
  • support@aqiri.org for technical support
  • privacy@aqiri.org for privacy matters
  • copyright@aqiri.org for copyright concerns
  • permissions@aqiri.org for permissions
  • security@aqiri.org for security issues

Contact Guidelines

Please include your full name, contact details, relevant department or program, a clear subject line, and any application or submission reference if available. AQIRI may not respond to incomplete, anonymous, abusive, or irrelevant messages.
