Before you submit

AQIRI welcomes original, properly referenced, and ethically prepared research for Al-Qamar and Al-Nasr.

Submissions must not be under review elsewhere or previously published. Authors should ensure that their manuscript is complete, anonymized for review, and consistent with the guidance below before beginning an online submission.

Important

Submitting a manuscript does not guarantee publication. Every paper passes through editorial screening and, where appropriate, double-blind peer review.

04 · Shared preparation checklist

Prepare your manuscript

LanguageEnglish, Urdu, or Arabic
File formatOpenOffice, Microsoft Word, or RTF document
PresentationSingle-spaced, 12-point text; use italics rather than underlining except for URLs
ReferencesFollow the selected journal’s current citation and reference instructions, and add URLs where available
Figures & tablesPlace illustrations, figures, and tables within the text at the appropriate points
AnonymityRemove author names, affiliations, and identifying information from the review file
OriginalityAll sources, translations, quotations, data, and prior work must be clearly acknowledged

Specific journal requirements may vary by issue or publication type. Authors should also consult the current instructions shown in the online journal portal.

05 · What happens next

Review process

1Editorial screening

Scope, completeness, originality, presentation, and basic ethical compliance.

2Double-blind review

Independent scholarly assessment with author and reviewer identities concealed.

3Author revision

Authors respond carefully to comments and provide a clear revision statement.

4Final decision

Acceptance, further revision, or rejection is communicated by the editorial office.

06 · Scholarly responsibility

Publication ethics

Authors are expected to uphold originality, accurate citation, honest authorship, confidentiality, and respectful scholarly engagement. Plagiarism, fabricated material, duplicate submission, undisclosed conflicts, and inappropriate authorship are not acceptable.

  • Credit every source and obtain permission for copyrighted material.
  • List only genuine contributors as authors.
  • Disclose funding and potential conflicts of interest.
  • Protect confidential, personal, or sensitive research material.
  • Cooperate promptly with corrections or editorial queries.
