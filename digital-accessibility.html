Digital Accessibility

This page explains AQIRI's approach to improving access to websites, online forms, journal pages, PDFs, publication files, and institutional digital resources.

Digital Platforms Covered

  • AQIRI official website and public pages
  • Journal websites and OJS platforms
  • Article, issue, and publication pages
  • PDF and downloadable files
  • Program, admission, career, and fellowship forms
  • Institutional notices, archives, and contact systems

Digital Access Priorities

  • Page readability and logical headings
  • Clear navigation and usable form fields
  • Descriptive buttons and links
  • Readable document presentation and mobile responsiveness
  • Correction of broken links and access to public information

Limitations and Help Requests

Some older or third-party content may have limitations due to scanned documents, legacy templates, external plugins, historical files, or uploaded author material. AQIRI may not be able to correct everything immediately, but it may provide reasonable support or alternative access where possible.

If you cannot access digital content, please send the page or document link, a short description of the difficulty, your preferred format or assistance needed, and any relevant deadline.
