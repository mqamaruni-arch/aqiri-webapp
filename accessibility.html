Accessibility Support

AQIRI aims to make its websites, publication pages, forms, notices, and digital resources more usable for readers, applicants, researchers, contributors, and the wider academic community.

Accessibility Commitment

  • Clear page headings and readable structure
  • Understandable website navigation
  • Accessible forms where possible
  • Readable text and sufficient contrast where possible
  • Descriptive links and labels
  • Mobile-friendly content where possible

Reporting an Accessibility Problem

If you experience difficulty accessing a page, form, file, article, journal issue, application system, or document, please contact AQIRI with the page URL or title, a description of the issue, your device or browser if relevant, and your contact information for follow-up.

Alternative Access

Where a file, form, or webpage is difficult to access, users may request an alternative format or assistance. Support depends on the type of content, available records, technical limitations, copyright restrictions, and institutional capacity.
