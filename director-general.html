Message from the Director General

At Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute, our work begins with a question central to Islamic scholarship today. How can a careful reading of the texts and debates we have inherited deepen our understanding of the issues we face now? We pursue that question through research, teaching, and publication, and by bringing scholars together to examine sources and discuss interpretations on which they may differ.

I welcome colleagues who have devoted many years to these fields and researchers just beginning to find their own questions. Whether you submit a manuscript, take part in a research project, or join one of our academic programmes, I hope you will find at AQIRI colleagues who read your work with care, challenge your ideas with respect, and help you take them further.

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Shahbaz Khan Manj
Director General, Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute, Lahore
