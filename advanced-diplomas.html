Advanced

Fall 2026 admissions are open until the end of November. Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute (AQIRI) offers advanced diplomas in two fields, with Spring and Fall intakes each year.

Apply for Fall 2026 →

Diploma 01

Advanced Diploma in Arabic Language and Islamic Texts

Read Qur’anic passages, Hadith texts, and selections from classical and contemporary Islamic writings with guidance. As you work through each passage, you will study its grammar and vocabulary, practise translating it into Urdu or English, and learn to use Arabic sources in your own research.

Diploma 02

Advanced Diploma in Academic Research and Scholarly Writing

Develop a research question and follow it through to a proposal, thesis chapter, or journal article. You will learn to review relevant scholarship, choose a suitable method, cite sources accurately, and revise your writing for submission.

Admissions Open

Fall 2026 Admissions

Students, graduates, researchers, teachers, and editors are welcome to apply. Select your preferred diploma on the admission form and include your educational background and a brief statement of interest.
