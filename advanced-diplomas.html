Program Overview

Practical skills for serious academic work.

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute offers advanced diploma programs for students, researchers, teachers, writers, editors, and early-career scholars who want structured academic training.

These programs strengthen practical skills through guided learning, reading-based study, supervised assignments, and applied scholarly practice.

Offered Programs

Two focused paths for academic development.

Program 01

Advanced Diploma in Arabic Language and Islamic Texts

For learners seeking stronger Arabic reading, grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, translation, and academic use of classical and contemporary Islamic texts.

Program Focus

  • Arabic grammar and sentence structure
  • Arabic reading and comprehension
  • Vocabulary development for Islamic texts
  • Translation from Arabic into Urdu or English
  • Reading Qur’anic and Hadith-related texts
  • Introduction to classical Islamic scholarly writings
  • Academic use of Arabic references and sources
  • Text-based exercises and guided reading practice

Suitable For

  • Students of Islamic Studies or Arabic
  • Researchers working with Arabic sources
  • Teachers and writers
  • MPhil and PhD scholars
  • Journal editors and academic assistants
  • Readers interested in classical Islamic texts

Learning Outcome

Participants should be able to read, understand, translate, and academically use Arabic texts with improved confidence, accuracy, and scholarly discipline.

Program 02

Advanced Diploma in Academic Research and Scholarly Writing

For students, researchers, writers, editors, and early-career academics developing skills in research planning, academic writing, referencing, methodology, and publication preparation.

Program Focus

  • Research topic selection
  • Research problem, objectives, questions, and methodology
  • Literature review writing
  • Academic writing style
  • Article and thesis writing support
  • References and citation management
  • Research ethics and plagiarism awareness
  • Use of academic databases and sources
  • Journal submission preparation
  • Revision and manuscript improvement

Suitable For

  • BS, MA, MPhil, and PhD students
  • Early-career researchers and university teachers
  • Research assistants
  • Journal editors and editorial interns
  • Academic writers
  • Students preparing proposals, theses, or articles

Learning Outcome

Participants should be able to plan, write, organize, reference, and present academic research according to proper scholarly standards.

Program Structure

Guided, practical, and applied learning.

Each diploma may include lectures, readings, assignments, practical exercises, supervised tasks, and final assessment. The exact structure may vary according to the announced schedule.

Participants may complete readings, translations, research exercises, writing tasks, assignments, or short projects. The aim is to develop usable academic skills for study, research, teaching, editing, and publication work.

ModeOnline / On-site / Hybrid
DurationAccording to the announced schedule
EligibilityStudents, graduates, researchers, teachers, and interested learners
MediumUrdu / English / Arabic, according to program requirements
AssessmentAssignments, participation, final task, or project
CertificateAwarded after successful completion
