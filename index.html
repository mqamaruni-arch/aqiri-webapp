Who we are

Islamic Research & Publication Since 2014

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute (AQIRI) advances Islamic Studies through research, publication, and academic training.

Based in Lahore, the Institute connects the Islamic scholarly tradition with contemporary questions and supports researchers, authors, and academic institutions.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To advance Islamic scholarship through rigorous research, responsible publishing, and researcher development.

Our Vision

Transforming global discourse through rigorous Islamic research.

Core Values

Academic Rigour

Sound methods, reliable sources, and appropriate scholarly review.

Contemporary Relevance

Engaging today’s questions with historical context and scholarly depth.

Intellectual Integrity

Ethical publishing, fair review, proper attribution, and academic independence.

Scholarly Empowerment

Training, mentorship, and fellowships that help researchers develop their work.

Fields of Inquiry

AQIRI supports research in Islamic studies and related humanities and social sciences.

Qur’an & Hadith

Qur’anic interpretation, Hadith sciences, and Seerah studies.

Islamic Law & Ethics

Fiqh, legal theory, Maqasid al-Shariah, and applied ethics.

Theology & Philosophy

Islamic beliefs, intellectual traditions, philosophy, and Sufism.

History & Culture

Islamic history, civilization, literature, and the arts.

Arabic & Textual Studies

Arabic language, manuscripts, translation, and classical texts.

Religion & Society

Religious traditions, lived religion, contemporary issues, and education.

Peace & Interfaith Studies

Religious dialogue, coexistence, and peacebuilding.

Economics & Governance

Islamic economics, finance, political thought, and public institutions.
