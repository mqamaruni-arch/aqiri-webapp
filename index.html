Who we are

A Scholarly Institution for Islamic Research and Publication Since 2014

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute is an academic research and publishing institute dedicated to Islamic Studies, scholarly development, and the promotion of serious research in Islamic thought and civilization.

The Institute provides a scholarly platform for researchers, teachers, students, authors, editors, and academic institutions to engage with Islam through authentic sources, classical scholarship, and modern research methods.

Vision and Objectives

Research, publication, and academic engagement in Islamic Studies and related fields.

Our Vision

AQIRI aims to contribute to the study, documentation, and publication of Islamic scholarship through research, academic training, and scholarly communication.

The institute is concerned with the intellectual traditions of Islam as well as the academic study of contemporary religious, social, ethical, legal, and cultural questions. Its work includes research support, peer-reviewed publication, academic programs, fellowships, and collaboration with scholars and institutions.

Objectives

Eight commitments guiding our academic work.

01

Support research in Islamic Studies

Encourage academic work in Qur’anic Studies, Hadith, Seerah, Islamic law, Islamic thought, Muslim history, civilization, ethics, comparative religion, and related fields.

02

Develop scholarly publications

Support journals, books, monographs, edited volumes, research reports, and other academic publications through organized editorial and publishing processes.

03

Maintain academic and ethical standards

Promote peer review, editorial responsibility, proper citation, research integrity, publication ethics, and transparent scholarly practice.

04

Provide academic training

Offer diplomas, workshops, research training, fellowships, internships, and learning opportunities related to research, writing, editing, and publishing.

05

Encourage interdisciplinary inquiry

Connect Islamic Studies with history, law, education, society, culture, ethics, interfaith studies, and contemporary academic debates.

06

Facilitate scholarly collaboration

Work with researchers, teachers, editors, reviewers, universities, research centers, journals, and academic contributors.

07

Improve access to research

Support digital publication, metadata quality, indexing readiness, open-access visibility, and organized preservation of scholarly work.

08

Contribute to informed academic discussion

Provide research, publications, programs, and scholarly resources supporting critical, ethical, and evidence-based engagement with relevant academic and social questions.

Institute Administration

Academic guidance and institutional oversight.

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute is administered through a structured system of academic guidance, institutional oversight, publication management, research coordination, and administrative support. Academic Programs

Research and advanced study opportunities

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute offers structured academic opportunities for researchers, graduates, teachers, scholars, and professionals who wish to strengthen their work in Islamic Studies, research methodology, academic writing, and scholarly publication.

The programs are designed to support serious study, guided research, manuscript development, and publication-oriented academic training.

Advanced Research

Postdoctoral Fellowships

Postdoctoral Fellowships are intended for PhD scholars and advanced researchers who wish to pursue independent or guided research in Islamic Studies and related fields.

Fellows may work on research articles, monographs, edited volumes, translations, manuscript studies, or thematic research projects in areas such as Qur’anic Studies, Hadith, Seerah, Islamic law, Islamic thought, Islamic civilization, comparative religion, and contemporary Muslim society.

Specialist Study

Advanced Diplomas

Advanced Diplomas are designed for graduates, teachers, researchers, and professionals who require focused academic training in Islamic Studies, research methods, scholarly writing, editing, citation, publication ethics, and academic publishing.

These programs may include seminars, supervised reading, guided assignments, research papers, manuscript development, and publication-oriented training.
