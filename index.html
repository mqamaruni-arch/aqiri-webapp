Who we are

A Scholarly Institution for Islamic Research and Publication Since 2014

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute (AQIRI) is an academic research and publishing institution working in Islamic Studies and related fields.

Established in 2014, the Institute supports research, scholarly publication, academic training, and specialised services for researchers and institutions. Its work is concerned both with the study of the Islamic scholarly tradition and with contemporary questions in religion, society, law, ethics, education, history, and intellectual life.

AQIRI works with researchers, authors, editors, teachers, students, and academic institutions to support serious scholarship, responsible publication, and sustained academic engagement.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute advances Islamic scholarship by producing scholarly literature, addressing contemporary issues, and equipping researchers through expert training and specialised services.

Our Vision

Transforming global discourse through rigorous Islamic research.

Our Core Purpose

We believe that classical Islamic scholarship holds essential answers for modern society. Al-Qamar Islamic Research Institute exists to make that knowledge visible, accessible, and rigorously applicable to today's global challenges.

Core Values

Academic Rigour

We maintain strict editorial integrity and classical scholarly standards across our journals, books, and contemporary media.

Our academic work is expected to be properly sourced, methodologically clear, critically informed, and subject to appropriate scholarly review.

Contemporary Relevance

We engage actively with modern challenges, ensuring traditional Islamic scholarship provides clear, meaningful insights for today's world.

AQIRI encourages research that addresses current questions without losing sight of historical context, established scholarship, and disciplinary method.

Intellectual Integrity

We ground our operations in transparency, fairness, and ethical publishing, providing a trustworthy platform for all researchers.

This includes responsible editorial practice, proper attribution, fair review, academic independence, and clear publication standards.

Scholarly Empowerment

We invest deeply in the future of the field by equipping next-generation researchers with expert training and specialized skills.

This commitment is reflected in AQIRI's training programmes, fellowships, mentorship, workshops, and academic support services.

Scholarly Focus

Fields of inquiry

AQIRI’s scholarly work spans the major disciplines of Islamic Studies, together with relevant areas of the humanities and social sciences.

01

Qur’anic Studies & Tafsir

Tafsir, Qur’anic sciences, interpretation, and the study of the Qur’an in classical and contemporary contexts.

02

Hadith & Sunnah Studies

Hadith sciences, transmission and criticism, major hadith collections, and studies of the Prophetic Sunnah.

03

Seerah & Prophetic Studies

The life and teachings of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, with attention to leadership, society, diplomacy, and historical context.

04

Fiqh & Islamic Jurisprudence

Classical and contemporary Islamic law, schools of jurisprudence, legal opinions, and emerging questions of Muslim life.

05

Usul al-Fiqh & Maqasid al-Shariah

Principles of Islamic legal reasoning, interpretation of legal sources, and the higher objectives of Islamic law.

06

Aqidah, Kalam & Islamic Theology

Islamic beliefs, theological schools and debates, classical theological writings, and contemporary questions of faith and doctrine.

07

Islamic Philosophy & Intellectual Thought

Islamic philosophy, Muslim intellectual traditions, knowledge and ethics, and the development of Islamic thought.

08

Tasawwuf & Islamic Spirituality

Sufi thought and traditions, spiritual practice, ethics, classical texts, major scholars, and historical development.

09

Islamic History & Civilization

Muslim societies, states and dynasties, institutions, intellectual movements, and Islamic civilization’s historical development.

10

Islamic Culture, Literature & Arts

Arabic, Persian, and Urdu literary traditions, alongside Islamic art, aesthetics, and cultural expression.

11

Arabic Language & Islamic Texts

Arabic language and linguistics, classical texts, textual studies, manuscripts, translation, and interpretation.

12

Study of Religions

Academic, historical, textual, philosophical, and social-scientific approaches to religion, religious traditions, beliefs, practices, institutions, and communities.

13

Anthropology of Religion

Religion in cultural and social life, ritual, symbolism, religious identity, lived religion, sacred spaces, indigenous traditions, and the interaction of religion with society and culture.

14

Peace & Interfaith Studies

Interfaith relations, religious coexistence, peacebuilding, conflict transformation, reconciliation, religious diplomacy, dialogue, and civilizational engagement.

15

Islam, Society & Contemporary Issues

Identity, family, gender, technology, social change, globalization, and Islam’s relationship with contemporary society.

16

Islamic Ethics & Applied Moral Studies

Islamic moral thought and contemporary ethical questions in medicine, the environment, professional life, and society.

17

Islamic Economics, Finance & Business Ethics

Islamic economic thought, banking and finance, commercial practices, entrepreneurship, and market ethics.

18

Islam, Governance & Political Thought

Islamic political thought, governance, public institutions, leadership, citizenship, and Muslim political experience.

19

Islamic Education & Pedagogy

Islamic educational thought, religious education, curriculum, teaching methods, learning practices, and institutions.

20

Related Humanities & Social Sciences

Connected research in history, literature, philosophy, linguistics, sociology, anthropology, law, education, and cultural studies.

Academic Programmes

Research and Advanced Study

AQIRI offers selected programmes for scholars, graduates, researchers, and professionals seeking advanced study, research development, and specialist academic training in Islamic Studies and related fields.

The programmes combine independent study with appropriate academic guidance and are designed around clearly defined scholarly outcomes, including research papers, extended studies, manuscripts, and other forms of academic work.

Advanced Research

Postdoctoral Fellowships

AQIRI’s Postdoctoral Fellowship Programme provides recent PhD graduates and qualified scholars with an institutional setting in which to develop a focused programme of postdoctoral research.

Fellows work under academic supervision on an approved research project in Islamic Studies or a related field. Depending on the nature of the project, the work may lead to research articles, a substantial study, a monograph, translation, critical edition, or other scholarly output.

The fellowship is intended to support sustained independent research, academic development, and scholarly publication during the fellowship period.

Specialist Study

Advanced Diplomas

AQIRI’s Advanced Diploma programmes provide focused study and practical academic training in areas relevant to Islamic scholarship and research.

Designed for graduates, researchers, teachers, and other suitably qualified participants, the programmes may combine taught sessions, supervised reading, written assignments, research exercises, and a substantial final piece of academic work.

Diplomas are offered in defined areas of study and are intended for participants seeking deeper subject knowledge or stronger skills in research, scholarly writing, and academic practice.
