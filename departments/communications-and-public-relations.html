Department Overview

Connecting the Institute with its academic community.

The Communications and Public Relations Department manages the public image, institutional communication, outreach, and visibility of the publisher. It connects the institute with authors, scholars, universities, readers, reviewers, partners, and the wider academic community.

The department prepares announcements, newsletters, social media content, publication updates, press releases, promotional campaigns, event communication, and institutional profiles. It also supports author publicity, journal promotion, book marketing, academic networking, and public engagement.

The department ensures that all public communication of the institute remains professional, accurate, consistent, and aligned with the academic identity of the publisher.

Core Responsibilities

Clear, credible, and consistent public communication.

01

Institutional Communication

Prepares official announcements, updates, public notices, newsletters, department profiles, and publication notices.

02

Public Relations

Maintains professional communication with authors, scholars, universities, reviewers, institutions, partners, media, and academic communities.

03

Publication Promotion

Supports the promotion of books, journals, monographs, edited volumes, reports, calls for papers, and new issues.

04

Social Media and Digital Outreach

Manages social content, publication updates, event posts, academic campaigns, visibility, and engagement.

05

Event Communication

Prepares communication for seminars, workshops, conferences, lectures, launches, and training programs.

06

Brand and Public Image

Maintains institutional tone, visual identity, communication style, and public presentation across print and digital platforms.

From communication need to public release.
