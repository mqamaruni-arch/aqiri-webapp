Compliance and Human Resource

Clear records and responsible internal operations.

The Compliance and Human Resource Department supports the internal administration of AQIRI through the management of personnel, institutional policies, appointments, records, and compliance procedures.

Its work is intended to ensure that staff and contributor arrangements are properly documented, institutional procedures are followed consistently, and administrative decisions are supported by clear records and appropriate internal controls.

The Department works across AQIRI’s academic, publishing, technical, and administrative units and provides support on matters relating to recruitment, appointments, contracts, staff records, institutional policy, and procedural compliance.

Department Responsibilities

Consistent support for institutional operations.

01

Human Resource Administration

The Department manages the principal records associated with AQIRI’s staff, interns, fellows, contractors, and other personnel.

This includes appointment documentation, personnel files, leave and attendance records, role information, official correspondence, and other employment or engagement-related documentation.

02

Recruitment and Appointments

The Department coordinates administrative aspects of recruitment and institutional appointments, including applications, shortlisting records, interview documentation, selection communication, appointment letters, and onboarding requirements.

Academic or technical selection decisions may involve the relevant department or academic authority, while the HR function maintains the formal administrative record.

03

Institutional Compliance

The Department supports the implementation and documentation of AQIRI’s internal policies, procedures, approvals, and institutional requirements.

Its role includes maintaining compliance records, reviewing whether required documentation is complete, and supporting departments in following established institutional procedures.

04

Contracts and Formal Documentation

The Department prepares and maintains administrative documentation connected with appointments, consultancy arrangements, service engagements, confidentiality requirements, undertakings, institutional agreements, and related matters.

Where specialist legal advice is required, the matter may be referred to an appropriate professional adviser.

05

Policy and Staff Guidance

The Department maintains internal policies and guidance relating to staff conduct, appointments, internships, work arrangements, documentation, and other administrative matters.

It also communicates relevant procedures and requirements to personnel where clarification or formal guidance is necessary.

06

Records and Administrative Coordination

Accurate institutional records are an important part of AQIRI’s administration.

The Department maintains personnel and compliance files, official correspondence, appointment records, internal notices, certificates, completion records, and other documentation required for institutional continuity and accountability.

07

Staff Development and Evaluation

The Department may support staff orientation, internship administration, professional development, and periodic evaluation in coordination with the relevant departmental leadership.

Its role is primarily administrative: academic, editorial, research, or technical performance is assessed with input from the appropriate supervising office.

Institutional Accountability

Administration beyond paperwork.

The purpose of the Department is not simply to maintain paperwork, but to provide a consistent administrative framework within which the Institute can operate responsibly.

Clear appointments, documented procedures, appropriate records, and transparent internal processes help protect both the institution and the individuals working with it.

Department Leadership

Compliance and human resource leadership.

The Compliance and Human Resource Department is led by the Director, Compliance and Human Resource, supported by personnel responsible for human resources, compliance, recruitment, records, and administrative coordination.

Staff profiles may be maintained separately through AQIRI’s institutional directory.

Administrative and HR Enquiries

Recruitment and institutional support.

The Department handles enquiries relating to recruitment, appointments, internships, personnel documentation, institutional policies, contracts, and other internal administrative matters.
