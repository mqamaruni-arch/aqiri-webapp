Department Overview

Order, accountability, and professional administration.

The Compliance and Human Resource Department manages the internal compliance, human resource, legal documentation, financial coordination, administrative records, and institutional policy functions of the publisher. It ensures that staff records, appointments, contracts, agreements, policies, compliance files, financial records, legal documents, and internal procedures are properly maintained.

The department supports recruitment, onboarding, staff coordination, internship records, appointment letters, leave records, performance documentation, financial files, invoices, payment records, contracts, legal correspondence, agreements, official notices, and institutional compliance matters. It works closely with all departments to keep internal operations organized, transparent, documented, and professionally managed.

The department plays an essential role in maintaining administrative order, staff discipline, legal record support, financial documentation, institutional accountability, and smooth coordination across publishing operations.

Core Responsibilities

Responsible management of people, policy, and records.

01

Human Resource Management

Manages staff and intern records, appointments, attendance, leave, assignments, performance notes, correspondence, and personnel files.

02

Compliance Management

Maintains compliance records, policies, procedures, approvals, agreements, notices, confidentiality records, and institutional documentation.

03

Legal Affairs and Documentation

Supports agreements, service terms, appointment conditions, undertakings, contracts, copyright records, policies, and legal correspondence.

04

Finance and Accounts Support

Maintains invoices, receipts, payment documentation, budgets, accounts coordination, correspondence, and financial records.

05

Recruitment and Onboarding

Supports recruitment, shortlisting, interviews, selection records, appointment letters, onboarding, and orientation documentation.

06

Staff Coordination

Coordinates directors, officers, associates, coordinators, assistants, interns, and contributors across internal workflows.

07

Policy and Documentation

Prepares HR policies, work guidelines, service terms, notices, evaluations, completion records, recommendations, and compliance notes.

08

Performance and Record Management

Maintains evaluations, reports, certificates, recommendations, disciplinary notes, legal files, financial files, and administrative records.

Administrative Workflow

Documented review from receipt to resolution.

  1. 01HR, Compliance, Legal, Finance, or Administrative Matter Received
  2. 02Record or File Created
  3. 03Relevant Policy, Requirement, Legal Document, or Financial Record Checked
  4. 04Internal Review Conducted
  5. 05Communication, Notice, Agreement, or Documentation Prepared
  6. 06Approval or Follow-up Completed
  7. 07Record Updated and Filed
  8. 08Matter Closed or Forwarded for Further Action
