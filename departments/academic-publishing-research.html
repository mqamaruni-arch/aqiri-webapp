Academic Focus

The Department of Academic Publishing and Research Development supports scholarly writing, research design, publication ethics, editorial training, citation practice, peer review awareness, journal development, and academic capacity building for Islamic Studies and related fields.

Its focus includes proposal development, literature review, methodology, referencing, plagiarism avoidance, manuscript preparation, journal submission standards, editorial workflow, indexing awareness, research reports, conference proceedings, book projects, and training manuals.

The department serves researchers, postgraduate students, faculty members, editors, reviewers, institutions, and journals through workshops, certificate courses, writing clinics, publication mentoring, editorial support, and research training programs.

Research Ethics and Publication Guidance

Contemporary guidance in this department centers on ethical research culture, transparent review, proper citation, responsible authorship, editorial accountability, academic language, data integrity, and meaningful contribution to knowledge.

The department helps scholars move from idea to proposal, from proposal to manuscript, and from manuscript to publication-ready submission. It encourages clear argument, careful evidence, balanced language, and respect for intellectual property.

Research and training themes may include publication ethics, journal policy, peer review, academic writing, citation management, research supervision, indexing preparation, book development, conference documentation, and institutional research capacity.

What to Include

Please mention your name, current qualification, phone or WhatsApp number, city and country, area of interest, proposed topic if any, preferred program, and the type of request you are sending.

For faster routing, write one clear subject such as course admission, research supervision, seminar invitation, workshop request, publication support, institutional collaboration, or general academic guidance.

The department will review the request and direct it to the relevant academic lead, research fellow, training coordinator, events coordinator, publication desk, or program assistant.
